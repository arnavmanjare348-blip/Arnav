## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies grounded mindfulness, uncompromising sustainability, and artisanal apothecarial luxury. It speaks to conscious consumers who seek ritualistic daily care, absolute ingredient transparency, and zero-waste packaging integrity without sacrificing sensory delight. The UI evokes warmth, purity, tactile presence, and quiet botanical confidence.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Apothecary Craft**: generous warm negative space, structural packaging-inspired framing lines, delicate natural textures, and refined editorial hierarchy. It avoids generic flat e-commerce tropes in favor of organic micro-details, soft paper-like tone layering, and clean botanical accents inspired by kraft pulp and amber glass.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in unbleached paper fibers, rich forest canopies, and living herbal foliage:
- **Primary (`#1E3F20` - Deep Botanical Forest):** Used for primary calls to action, high-emphasis header accents, key category tags, and grounded UI anchors.
- **Secondary (`#4E8A3C` - Fresh Sprout Green):** Used for active indicators, successful eco-metric progress, micro-accents, and botanical badges.
- **Tertiary (`#2E5A27` - Moss Green):** Acts as an intermediary tone for borders, hover states, secondary icons, and botanical subheadings.
- **Neutral (`#1F2421` - Warm Charcoal):** The primary text and high-contrast element color, preventing harsh synthetic black while ensuring accessibility.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#F7F4EE` (Raw Kraft Linen)
  - Card & Container surface: `#EFE9DD` (Pressed Oatmeal Kraft)
  - Subtle borders & divider lines: `#D8CEBC` (Fiber Grain Taupe)

## Typography
The typographic voice pairs editorial poise with crisp scannability:
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Brings bespoke artisanal luxury and historical herbalist authority. Used for product titles, value proposition statements, and feature callouts. Italic cuts are reserved for botanical names, key ingredients (e.g., *Ocimum sanctum*), and packaging sub-captions.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a contemporary, friendly, high-legibility counterpart. Its open counters and geometric balance maintain flawless clarity across ingredient listings, brewing guidelines, and nutritional breakdowns.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges:** Upper-case treatment with open letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) is used for botanical certification badges, batch identifiers, and packaging spec labels.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a harmonious, proportional fluid grid structured around 12 columns on desktop (`>= 1024px`), 8 columns on tablet (`>= 640px`), and 4 columns on mobile:
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing scales along multiples of 4px and 8px, prioritizing generous vertical air around key editorial storytelling blocks and tighter packaging-spec grids for ingredient tables and certifications.
- **Layout Behavior:** Product showcases borrow grid layouts from packaging labels: thin internal separating lines (`1px solid #D8CEBC`) segmenting instruction steps, botanical specifications, and sustainability badges.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Desktop layouts display asymmetrical product visuals alongside rigid specifications; mobile devices stack content cleanly into single-column containers with standardized `margin-sm` (16px) margins.

## Elevation & Depth
In line with the brand's tactile craft and zero-waste ethos, depth is rendered through **tonal paper layering** and **delicate hairline boundaries** rather than synthetic diffuse drop shadows:
- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#F7F4EE`. Elevation tier 1 components (cards, product modules) sit on `#EFE9DD`. Popovers, floating bars, and modal sheets elevate to `#FAF8F3`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Surfaces are framed with subtle 1px fiber borders (`#D8CEBC`), mimicking die-cut kraft paper boxes and apothecary label borders.
- **Ambient Herbarium Shadow (Interactive Only):** For floating carts or active modal overlays, use an extremely low-opacity botanical-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 63, 32, 0.08)`. Harsh black shadows are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors sustainable packaging die-cuts: gentle, tactile, soft corners that feel human and organic rather than sterile or synthetic.
- **Base Rounding (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive components (buttons, input fields, badges) feature a soft `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius.
- **Cards & Modules:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) to emulate the edges of folded unbleached cartons and tea pouches.
- **Pill Badges:** Botanical certification marks and origin tags use fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to stand out as natural stamp seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E3F20`, text `#F7F4EE`, 1px border `#1E3F20`. On hover: background `#2E5A27`, gentle micro-lift (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, text `#1E3F20`, 1.5px border `#1E3F20`. On hover: background `#EFE9DD`.
- **Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#1E3F20`. On hover: text `#4E8A3C`, underline offset 4px.

### Cards & Packaging Panels
- **Product Card:** Surface `#EFE9DD`, 1px solid `#D8CEBC` border, 8px corner radius. Features an image container framed by subtle inner hairline padding, an editorial headline (`Playfair Display`), clear weight/size specifications, and bottom-aligned price and add action.
- **Spec Grid Card (Packaging Back Metaphor):** Clean cell layout divided by `1px solid #D8CEBC` horizontal and vertical lines. Used for "Brewing Instructions", "How to Use", and "Key Ingredients".

### Eco-Badges & Chips
- **Certification Badges:** Outline style with 1px border `#D8CEBC`, surface `#F7F4EE`, text `#1E3F20`. Accompanied by minimalist line icons (e.g., leaf, compost mark, rabbit).
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive: border `#D8CEBC`, text `#1F2421`, surface `transparent`. Active: background `#1E3F20`, text `#F7F4EE`, border `#1E3F20`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#F7F4EE`, border 1px solid `#D8CEBC`, text `#1F2421`, placeholder `#8C867A`. Focus state: border 1.5px solid `#1E3F20`, no outer halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Border 1.5px solid `#2E5A27`, background `#FAF8F3`. When selected: background `#1E3F20` with a sharp white botanical checkmark.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Ritual Guide (Steep / Use Step Flow):** Three-stage horizontal step component with minimalist line icons, steep temperatures, and duration tickers.
- **Ingredient Transparency Drawer:** Side-sheet detailing the botanical origin, Latin nomenclature, and sustainability score of every raw compound.
